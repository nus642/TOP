# Problem Background

During live tournaments, referee calls from the tournament director may not be heard by referees or players because of:

- large venues
- multiple courts operating simultaneously
- noisy environments
- players focusing on matches


# Current Process

Tournament director uses:

- voice announcements
- manual communication


# Pain Point

Information delivery is unreliable.

The correct participant may not receive:

- when to play
- which court
- match readiness status


# Future Concept

Digital match calling and court notification.

Possible future capabilities:

- player notification
- referee notification
- court queue display
- match readiness status
- live operation dashboard


# Domain Relationship

Future capability belongs to:

Match Operations domain.

Relationship:

Schedule
    |
Match Queue
    |
Calling / Notification
    |
Court Operation
    |
Match Result
