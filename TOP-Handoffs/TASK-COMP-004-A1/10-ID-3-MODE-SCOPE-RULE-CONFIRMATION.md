# 1. Purpose

This document prepares the domain rule confirmation for ID-3. It gives a confirming domain representative a bounded way to clarify the competition modes and contest contexts to which the meaning confirmed through ID-1, and its interpretation through change confirmed through ID-2, apply.

This artifact does not answer ID-3. The modes and contexts below are discussion prompts, not a selected confirmation scope. No listed interpretation is a preferred rule, and no example classifies an outcome.

ID-3 concerns bounded applicability only. It does not redefine contest identity, redefine continuity meaning, or assign ownership of external competition operations.

# 2. Relationship with ID-1 and ID-2

The three confirmation questions are ordered and distinct:

| Decision | Role in domain rule confirmation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| ID-1 | Defines meaning. | Confirms what business meaning makes a contest the same contest. |
| ID-2 | Applies meaning through change. | Confirms how the ID-1 meaning is interpreted when permitted business changes occur. |
| ID-3 | Clarifies where the meaning applies. | Confirms whether that meaning and interpretation apply across competition modes or only within a bounded applicability. |

ID-3 must use the meaning already confirmed through ID-1 and the continuity interpretation already confirmed through ID-2. A mode difference cannot be used in ID-3 to invent a different contest identity test or a different continuity outcome. If a context cannot be assessed without doing either, that context remains unresolved or is recorded as a bounded deferral.

A broad ID-3 confirmation would establish only that the same confirmed meaning applies in the stated confirmation scope. A bounded confirmation would identify where that meaning applies and where further domain rule confirmation is still required. Neither form transfers responsibility for facts or external operations.

# 4. Competition Mode Scope Boundary

The ID-3 confirmation scope asks where the already-confirmed meaning applies. It may consider the following examples without selecting a mode policy or classifying any continuity outcome:

| Mode or context example | Scope point to clarify | What must remain open in this artifact |
|---|---|---|
| Individual competitions | Whether contests involving individual competitors fall within the same confirmation scope. | Whether the ID-1 meaning applies, and whether any bounded clarification is required. |
| Team encounters | Whether a parent team encounter, its constituent contests, or both fall within the confirmation scope. | How the confirmed meaning applies at either level and whether their interpretations differ. |
| Round-robin | Whether contests established within round-robin structure fall within the confirmation scope. | Whether structural position requires bounded clarification. |
| Elimination | Whether contests established within elimination structure fall within the confirmation scope. | Whether progression-dependent context requires bounded clarification. |
| Generated or provisional contests | Whether generated contests whose competing sides or context are not yet fully settled fall within the confirmation scope. | Whether provisional status is recognized for this purpose and how the confirmed meaning applies. |

These examples may overlap. For example, a team encounter may occur within a round-robin or elimination context, and an individual contest may initially be provisional. The confirmation must therefore describe bounded applicability clearly enough to avoid assuming that one label settles another scope dimension.

Being inside the ID-3 confirmation scope does not determine the answer to any particular continuity scenario. Being outside it does not establish a different rule; it records that the existing domain rule confirmation has not yet been extended to that context.

# 6. Scenario Prompts

These prompts test the clarity of the confirmation scope. They do not answer ID-3 and do not classify contest identity or continuity outcomes.

## Scenario 1: Individual round-robin contest

**Mode/context:** An individual competition uses round-robin structure, and a contest has been generated within that structure.

**Competing interpretations:**
- The confirmed meaning may apply to the contest without further mode clarification.
- The combination of individual competition and round-robin structure may require bounded clarification before applicability can be confirmed.

**Confirmation question:** Is this combined context within the confirmation scope, without changing the meaning confirmed through ID-1?

## Scenario 2: Parent team encounter and constituent contests

**Mode/context:** A team encounter contains multiple constituent contests.

**Competing interpretations:**
- The confirmation scope may cover both the parent encounter and each constituent contest.
- Applicability may need to be confirmed separately for the parent and constituent levels while preserving one established set of boundaries.

**Confirmation question:** Which contest levels are within bounded applicability, and what remains deferred?

## Scenario 3: Elimination contest with participants not yet known

**Mode/context:** An elimination structure contains a generated contest whose participants depend on earlier outcomes.

**Competing interpretations:**
- The generated contest may already be within the confirmation scope despite unsettled participants.
- Applicability may remain bounded until the business confirms how provisional context relates to the ID-1 meaning.

**Confirmation question:** Is this generated or provisional contest in scope, or does it require an explicit bounded deferral?

## Scenario 4: Team encounter in round-robin structure

**Mode/context:** A team competition uses round-robin structure for its encounters.

**Competing interpretations:**
- A confirmation covering team encounters may be sufficient for this context.
- The overlapping team and round-robin dimensions may require a more precise statement of confirmation scope.

**Confirmation question:** Does the stated scope cover this overlap clearly, without creating a mode-specific identity rule?

## Scenario 5: Individual elimination contest after progression

**Mode/context:** An individual elimination contest becomes fully specified after earlier competition outcomes are known.

**Competing interpretations:**
- The confirmed meaning may apply throughout both the provisional and fully specified contexts.
- Only one of those contexts may currently fall within bounded applicability.

**Confirmation question:** Which contexts are covered by the same confirmed meaning, and which require further confirmation?

## Scenario 6: Similar change in individual and team contexts

**Mode/context:** The same kind of permitted business change is observed once in an individual contest and once in a constituent contest within a team encounter.

**Competing interpretations:**
- Both contexts may be governed by the same ID-1 meaning and ID-2 interpretation.
- The team or individual context may expose an applicability question that has not yet been confirmed.

**Confirmation question:** Are both contexts inside the ID-3 confirmation scope, or must one remain a bounded deferral without selecting different outcomes?
