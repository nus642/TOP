## Purpose

This guide helps players participate smoothly in a TOP-supported tournament. It focuses on preparation, check-in, match readiness, score confirmation, communication, and issue escalation.

---

## Player Responsibilities

| Area | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | Confirm that tournament entry, division, and contact information are correct before the event. |
| Readiness | Arrive on time, monitor official announcements, and be ready when assigned matches are called. |
| Rule awareness | Review the published format, scoring rules, venue rules, and organizer instructions. |
| Score confirmation | Verify match results before they are submitted or published. |
| Communication | Use official tournament channels for questions, conflicts, withdrawals, or corrections. |
| Conduct | Treat opponents, referees, organizers, staff, and spectators respectfully. |

---

## Before the Event

1. Confirm your registration, player name, team name if applicable, and division.
2. Review the tournament format, match rules, scoring method, and no-show policy.
3. Check the event schedule, venue location, equipment expectations, and arrival time.
4. Save the official communication channel provided by the organizer.
5. Notify the organizer early if your availability, contact information, or registration status changes.

---

## Check-In

During check-in:

- Arrive within the organizer's published check-in window.
- Confirm your identity, division, and player or team status.
- Ask staff where official match assignments and schedule updates will be posted.
- Report any registration, eligibility, or schedule issue before matches begin.

Players who miss check-in may be delayed, replaced, marked absent, or handled according to the published event policy.

---

## Match Readiness

When a match is assigned:

1. Confirm the opponent, match location, scheduled time, and referee if assigned.
2. Arrive at the assigned location before the expected start time.
3. Bring required equipment, uniform, identification, or team materials.
4. Confirm the match format with the referee or organizer if anything is unclear.
5. Stay near the match area until the result has been confirmed.

Avoid relying on informal schedule information from other players. The official schedule, organizer announcement, or referee assignment should be treated as authoritative.

---

## During a Match

Players should:

- Follow the published tournament rules and referee instructions.
- Track the score carefully and raise questions promptly.
- Keep communication respectful and brief during active play.
- Pause only when rules, safety, equipment, or score concerns require attention.
- Avoid arguing with opponents after a referee or organizer has taken ownership of an issue.

---

## Score Confirmation

At the end of a match:

1. Confirm the final score with the opponent and referee or authorized scorekeeper.
2. Check winner, loser, score order, and any tie-break details before leaving.
3. Report a score issue immediately through the official correction path.
4. Do not assume standings or next matches are final until the organizer publishes or confirms them.

Score corrections are easiest when reported immediately, before the next dependent match or standings update.

---

## Schedule Updates

Tournament schedules may change because of late arrivals, long matches, venue issues, disputes, or organizer decisions.

Players should:

- Monitor the official schedule or announcement channel regularly.
- Be ready for earlier or later match calls when the organizer announces changes.
- Ask staff for clarification if two posted sources appear inconsistent.
- Notify the organizer immediately if a schedule conflict prevents timely arrival.

---

## Disputes and Escalations

| Situation | Player Action |
| --- | --- |
| Score disagreement | Pause promptly, reconstruct the score with the opponent, and involve the referee if unresolved. |
| Rule disagreement | Ask the referee or organizer to apply the published tournament rules. |
| Schedule conflict | Contact the organizer through the official channel as soon as the conflict is known. |
| Equipment or venue issue | Notify the referee or organizer before continuing if play quality or safety is affected. |
| Conduct concern | Avoid direct confrontation and report the concern to the referee or organizer. |
| Incorrect published result | Report the match, players, expected correction, and supporting details immediately. |

Escalations should include the match identifier if available, player names, location, time, and a concise description of the issue.

---

## Withdrawals and No-Shows

If you cannot continue in the event:

1. Notify the organizer as early as possible.
2. Confirm whether the withdrawal affects a current match, future match, bracket, or standings.
3. Follow the organizer's official withdrawal or forfeit process.
4. Avoid leaving the venue without confirming your status if you still have active assignments.

No-show decisions are handled by the organizer according to the published tournament policy.

---

## Communication Guidelines

- Use official channels instead of side conversations for important decisions.
- Keep messages specific: include your name, division, match, and issue.
- Be respectful when asking for clarification or correction.
- Do not pressure referees or staff to make unofficial changes.
- Preserve screenshots, score notes, or relevant details when reporting an issue.

---

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] Rules, format, and schedule reviewed.
- [ ] Check-in completed.
- [ ] Official communication channel monitored.
- [ ] Match assignment confirmed before play.
- [ ] Final score verified after each match.
- [ ] Issues escalated promptly through official channels.
