Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

## Purpose

This manual helps referees operate consistently during a TOP-supported tournament. It focuses on match-day responsibilities, score handling, issue escalation, and coordination with organizers.

---

## Referee Responsibilities

| Area | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Match readiness | Confirm court/table/field, player presence, equipment, and match format before play starts. |
| Rule enforcement | Apply the tournament rules and format communicated by the organizer. |
| Score accuracy | Record scores immediately and verify them before submission. |
| Incident handling | Pause play when safety, rule, or conduct issues require organizer review. |
| Communication | Keep players informed of decisions and notify organizers of unresolved issues. |

---

## Before the Match

1. Confirm the assigned match, players, and location.
2. Review the match format, scoring rules, and any organizer-specific instructions.
3. Check that equipment and playing conditions are ready.
4. Confirm both players or teams are present.
5. Explain any local procedures, such as score confirmation or dispute escalation.

---

## During the Match

- Track the score continuously.
- Apply rules neutrally and consistently.
- Communicate decisions clearly and briefly.
- Stop play if a safety issue, equipment failure, or unresolved rule dispute occurs.
- Avoid changing match format or tournament rules without organizer approval.

---

## Score Submission

1. Confirm the final score with both sides.
2. Enter or report the score using the organizer-approved process.
3. Double-check winner, loser, and score order before submission.
4. Notify the organizer immediately if the submitted score is incorrect or disputed.

---

## Disputes and Incidents

| Situation | Referee Action |
| --- | --- |
| Score disagreement | Pause play, reconstruct the score with players, and escalate if unresolved. |
| Rule disagreement | Apply the published tournament rules or request organizer clarification. |
| Player conduct issue | Warn when appropriate and report serious conduct concerns to the organizer. |
| Safety issue | Stop play and notify the organizer before continuing. |
| Technical issue | Record what happened and ask the organizer for the official correction path. |

---

## Communication Guidelines

- Stay neutral and concise.
- Explain decisions based on the rule or tournament instruction.
- Do not debate once a decision has been escalated.
- Record important incident details while they are fresh.

---

## Handoff to Organizer

At the end of an assignment, provide the organizer with:

- Completed match scores.
- Any unresolved disputes.
- Incident notes.
- Equipment or venue concerns.
- Matches that may need schedule adjustment.

---

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] Match assignment confirmed.
- [ ] Players or teams verified.
- [ ] Rules and format reviewed.
- [ ] Score tracked and confirmed.
- [ ] Result submitted.
- [ ] Incidents escalated or documented.

## Match-Day Quick Card（赛日快速卡 · 裁判）

适用首场赛事：手机浏览器访问 `http://服务器IP:3000/operator/`（建议添加到主屏幕）。

### 进入工作台

1. 打开页面，在“裁判身份入口”输入赛事编号。
2. 从花名册下拉中选择本人姓名，点击“进入裁判工作台”。
3. 页面显示已分配的比赛卡片；无卡片表示暂无派单，下拉/刷新页面即可。

### 三步执裁流程（每场比赛）

| 步骤 | 操作 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 点击“接受执裁任务” | 收到主控派单后尽快接单；无法执裁请立即告知主控撤回改派。 |
| 2 | 点击“开始比赛” | 确认双方选手到场后开赛。 |
| 3 | 录入比分并结束执行 | 录入让分后的最终比分（让分在场下处理，系统只记最终结果），提交后等待主控确认。 |

场地受限/需要中断时：卡片上会出现“中断比赛”按钮；主控报告场地恢复后点击“恢复比赛”。

### 故障应对

| 故障 | 处理 |
| --- | --- |
| 页面提示登录失效 / 服务器刚重启 | 重新打开页面，从身份入口重新选择本人姓名进入；比赛数据不会丢失。 |
| 看不到派给自己的任务 | 确认赛事编号正确；联系主控确认已派单（必要时撤回重派）。 |
| 比分录错 | 立即联系主控，由主控按官方更正流程处理，不要自行重复提交。 |
