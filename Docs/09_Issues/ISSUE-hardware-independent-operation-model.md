# Issue: Hardware Independent Tournament Operation Model

## Background

During the development and operation of the Legacy system, we identified an important product opportunity.

Many existing tournament operation systems rely on dedicated hardware devices or specific court-side equipment.

This approach creates additional requirements:

* Hardware purchasing cost
* Hardware deployment
* Hardware maintenance
* Technical personnel support during events

For many tournament organizers, this increases operational complexity and cost.

---

## Observation

TOP has an opportunity to provide a different operational model:

> Tournament operation should be software-driven and hardware-independent.

The goal is:

* Referees do not depend on dedicated hardware
* Tournament masters can operate the system with standard devices
* Event setup should be simple and repeatable
* The system should be usable without dedicated technical support staff

---

## Product Principle

TOP should maintain the principle:

> Master can open the system and start tournament operations immediately.

The platform should minimize:

* Configuration complexity
* Hardware dependency
* Technical maintenance requirements

---

## Commercial Impact

The Super Admin concept is not only a technical role.

It represents a future business model:

* Platform owner manages system access
* Super Admin authorizes a tournament Master
* Master operates an individual tournament

Potential delivery model:

Platform subscription / tournament authorization.

---

## Future Considerations

Possible future capabilities:

* Tournament license management
* Master account authorization
* Event-based access control
* Subscription management
* Usage-based pricing

---

## Status

Strategic product direction.

Not part of current MVP implementation.
